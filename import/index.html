Bring your system with you

Anchor Plural reads the export files of six other apps for plural systems. The file is read on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account to make first.

How it works

You need the export file you saved from the other app. In Anchor Plural, tap “I’m okay, open the system”, then Settings, then Import, and choose that file.

The app says what it found in the file and asks before it changes anything. “Add to what is here” keeps everything already on the phone and adds what the file has. Importing the same file twice changes nothing.

If the other app has shut down, the export file you saved is what the import needs. Anchor Plural cannot fetch anything from a server, the other app’s or anyone else’s.

What comes across

Simply Plural

From the data file: members and custom fronts, groups with the groups inside them, custom fields and each member’s values, and the full front history with its comments. Notes become journal pages. Board messages become notes between members. Chat messages and polls come too.

Photos were a separate avatar download in Simply Plural. Import the data file first, then choose the avatar file the same way, and each picture goes to its member.

PluralKit

Members with their pronouns, colour and description, and every switch as front history, co-fronting included. Photos do not come: that file holds web addresses of pictures, and the app fetches nothing.

Octocon

Members, tags as groups, custom fields and front history.

Ampersand

From a .json, .ampdb or .ampar file: members with their photos, custom fields, tags and systems as groups, front history and journal posts.

Plural Star

From a .json or .zip file: members with their photos, groups, custom fields, front history, the journal, notes between members and polls.

Plural Log

From its .zip file: members with their photos, groups, front history, the journal and notes between members.

Taking it with you again

Settings has two exports. “Export as a file” saves everything, photos included, in one file that Anchor Plural reads back whole. “Export for other apps” writes members, groups and front history in PluralKit’s format, which other plural apps can read.

If something did not come across

Tell us at support@anchorplural.org which app the file came from and what is missing. Please do not send the file itself: it holds your system’s information, and we would rather not have it.

This is ordinary email, not a crisis service. If you need someone now, use the “I need to talk to someone” button in the app or call your local emergency number.
